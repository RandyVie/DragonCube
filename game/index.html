How to play

  1. Pick your stake with the coin button and press play. The stake is paid once per game.
  2. The dragon rolls a column of 3 dice. You choose which of the 4 fields it goes into. The column fills the next free column of that field, left to right. You get 12 columns.
  3. 3 equal dice on a row or a diagonal of a field is a winning line (5 lines per field).
  4. Wins in 3 fields: points ×1.5. Wins in all 4 fields: points ×2. A field of 9 equal dice: +200.
  5. Reach 100 points or more to win a prize from the ladder.
  6. Summon the dragon: every die (1 to 7 stars) that forms a winning line is collected in the meter. Collect all 7 and the dragon is summoned after the game. Your collection is kept between games, separately for each stake.
  7. The dragon grants one wish: 10 Free Games, Dragon's Treasure (take it or not: up to 5 offers, the 5th must be taken) or Dragon Wilds (5 free games where dragon dice count as any face). Nothing is collected during free games.

Tip: the glowing field is the dragon's suggestion. Pressing play (or space) while choosing places the dice there. The round arrow button starts autoplay.
